## Orthogonal adaptation prior art (additional primary-source check)

[OFT, Qiu et al., NeurIPS 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07280)
already uses orthogonal transformations for adaptation and adds a constraint for
stability. [PSOFT, Wu et al., ICLR 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11235)
confines transformations to a principal subspace of pretrained weights and relaxes
orthogonality with tunable vectors. Official current abstracts were inspected;
these methods were not reproduced here. Orthogonality, low-dimensional subspaces,
and learned adaptation are therefore insufficient novelty claims for QC-B3.
Our runtime value-indexed action and local replacement experiment require a
separate, detailed comparison; these abstracts do not establish equivalence or
novelty of the entire proposed lifecycle architecture.
